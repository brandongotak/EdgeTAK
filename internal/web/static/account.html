My certificates

Each device that enrolled gets its own certificate. If you lose a device, revoke its certificate so it can't connect. To renew one that is expiring, enroll the device again (sign in again in the app, or import a new connection package).

Change password

Devices keep working with their certificates. Apps that sign in with your password, such as when they enroll again or download data packages, need the new one.
